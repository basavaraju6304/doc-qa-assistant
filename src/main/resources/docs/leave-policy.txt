Acme Company Leave Policy

Annual Leave
Every full-time employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per year. Unused annual leave up to 10 days can be carried over to the next year. Leave must be requested at least 7 days in advance through the HR portal.

Sick Leave
Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required if sick leave lasts more than 2 consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried over.

Work From Home
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Work from home is not allowed during the first 30 days after joining.

Notice Period
The standard notice period for resignation is 60 days. Employees in their probation period have a notice period of 15 days. The company may allow an early release with written approval from the department head.

Maternity and Paternity Leave
Female employees get 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Male employees get 2 weeks of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth.
